BEEPY — MAP SERVICE AND VEHICLE ILLUSTRATIONS
Prepared 8 November 2026 for App Review, app version 1.0.0.

1. Yandex Maps
Beepy uses the official Yandex MapKit SDK for map display, place search and geocoding. The developer's API console was inspected on 8 November 2026: MapKit SDK was Active, on the Free plan. The console displayed 2 out of 1,000 requests used that day. No plan change or purchase was made.
Applicable public terms:
https://yandex.ru/legal/maps_api/ru/
https://yandex.ru/legal/maps_termsofuse/ru/

Beepy is a free public app. The latest source changes add tappable links to both terms under More > About. Geocoded trip addresses use a 29-day cache; expired entries and legacy entries without a retrieval date are cleared at launch/foreground and during active use. Expired addresses are excluded when creating trip display data. Trip distances and user-recorded route coordinates are not removed by this cache cleanup. iOS cannot run cleanup while the app is suspended; it resumes at the next activation.
These corrections are for build 5; build 4 did not include them.

2. Vehicle illustrations
The vehicle catalogue contains 181 HEIC images. The repository retains generation tools and a dated development journal. Its final 6 September generation entry records Gemini/Nano Banana via OpenRouter; later entries document further generation batches. Local original PNG outputs are retained. Earlier records also describe Kandinsky experiments/batches; the audit does not certify the exact model used for each individual shipped file.

The developer explicitly confirmed on 8 November 2026 that the original Lexus reference was also generated by them with Nano Banana. This is a developer-provided provenance statement, not a licence certificate issued by a car manufacturer. The illustrations should not be described as official manufacturer photographs or endorsements.

Relevant provider terms:

OpenRouter defers generated-output rights to the relevant model terms. Google states it does not claim ownership of generated content. These terms do not certify third-party trademark rights or replace rights required for input materials.
