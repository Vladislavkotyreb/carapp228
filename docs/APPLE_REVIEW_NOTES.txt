2. Purpose and intended audience

Beepy is a free journal for car owners: maintenance records, mileage, trips, service locations and on-device engine sound analysis. Analysis is preliminary, requires verification by a mechanic and does not guarantee vehicle safety.

Beepy is for the general public. It has no subscriptions, purchases, paid features, ads, public posts or messaging. Records remain private.

3. Access and feature walkthrough

Open Beepy and tap “Войти с Apple” (Sign in with Apple). Use an Apple Account through Apple's system sign-in flow. There is no Beepy password, invitation or approval step. Name/email are not requested. No sample files or extra hardware are needed.

Add a vehicle manually by name. In “Машина” (Car), enter mileage and add maintenance records. The app uses those records to display maintenance information. License-plate lookup is unavailable in this release; manual vehicle entry is available without a subscription or car-count limit.

Open the map to explore car service locations and save favorites. Internet access is needed for map data. Location permission supports location-related features.

Open “Ошибки” (Diagnostics), allow microphone access, and follow the instructions to record engine sound. Analysis runs on the iPhone. A real engine recording is needed for a meaningful result.

In “Ещё” (More), open trip/mileage settings to use optional trip tracking. Location permissions are needed for tracking; background location is used when that functionality is enabled. Trips and vehicle records are stored on the device. Cloud synchronization is unavailable in this release.

“Ещё” also provides sign-out and “Удалить аккаунт и данные” (Delete account and data). Deletion clears the local account session and vehicle, trip, maintenance and favorite-place data. The app then provides instructions for revoking Sign in with Apple access in Apple Account settings. Apple authorization revocation is a separate manual step; it is not performed automatically by a Beepy server.

4. External services and technologies

- Apple Sign in with Apple: system authentication; the local session identifier is stored in Keychain.
- Yandex Maps / MapKit: map display, location search and geocoding. Map data requires connectivity.
- Apple Core Location: location and optional trip/mileage tracking.
- Apple Core ML: on-device engine sound analysis. No external cloud AI service processes recordings in this release.
- GitHub Pages: public support and privacy-policy pages.

There is no active Beepy account backend, payment processor, cloud synchronization or license-plate data-provider integration in the submitted release.

5. Regional behavior

The Russian-language app has no intentional region-specific feature or pricing differences. Local features work consistently across regions. Map coverage/results depend on Yandex service availability and data; coverage is not identical worldwide. Sign-in depends on Apple service availability.

6. Third-party materials

Beepy is a personal vehicle journal, not a regulated financial or medical service. It uses Yandex Maps and vehicle imagery. The developer has confirmed that the necessary usage rights are held.
